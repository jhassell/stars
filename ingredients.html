Lesson 1: Ingredients & Prep

The difference between a homemade burger and a Big Mac clone comes down to precision on each component. Here is the exact shopping list with weights.

Beef

  • Grade: USDA Choice or Prime, 80/20 lean-to-fat ratio
  • Weight per patty: 1.6 oz (45 g) — this is lighter than a quarter-pounder (4 oz) by design
  • Total beef per sandwich: 3.2 oz (90 g)
  • Forming: Loose ball, no binding agents, no salt until the griddle
  • Why 80/20: Leaner beef (90/10) dries out in 90 seconds; fattier (70/30) flares excessively on a 400 °F surface

Special Sauce (makes ~1 cup / 240 ml, enough for 10 burgers)

IngredientWeightVolume
Mayonnaise120 g1/2 cup
Sweet pickle relish30 g2 tbsp
Yellow mustard15 g1 tbsp
White vinegar5 g1 tsp
Garlic powder1.5 g1/2 tsp
Onion powder1.5 g1/2 tsp
Paprika1 g1/4 tsp
Black pepper0.5 g1/8 tsp

Whisk thoroughly; refrigerate ≥30 minutes before use so flavors marry. Per burger: 1.5 tbsp (22 ml).

Buns (Sesame Seed, 3.75 in / 9.5 cm diameter)

  • Top bun (crown): One per sandwich
  • Middle bun (club/heel): One per sandwich — this is the signature third bun
  • Bottom bun (heel): One per sandwich
  • Weight each: ~35 g
  • Toast: Butter cut sides, griddle 30–45 seconds until golden

Produce & Cheese

  • Iceberg lettuce: Hand-shredded to 1/8 in (3 mm) strips; 15 g per burger — food processor bruises cells and releases water
  • American cheese: 2 slices (approx. 21 g total), placed on each patty during the last 15 seconds of cook
  • Dill pickle chips: 3 slices (Vlasic or Claussen, 1/4 in thick), placed on middle bun
  • Yellow onion: Finely diced, 0.2 oz (6 g) per burger — soak in ice water 5 minutes to mellow bite, drain well

Prep Timeline (for 4 burgers)

  1. T-30 min: Make sauce, refrigerate
  2. T-15 min: Portion beef into 8 balls (1.6 oz each), cover, room temp
  3. T-10 min: Shred lettuce, dice onion (ice-water soak), slice pickles
  4. T-5 min: Butter buns, slice cheese if not pre-sliced
  5. T-0: Heat griddle to 400 °F (204 °C) — infrared gun recommended
